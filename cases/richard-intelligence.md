# Richard Carlson’s connections to intelligence agencies

What records define Richard Carlson’s capacity, responsibilities and dealings with named agencies during his broadcasting and diplomatic work?

**Richard’s own account.** His 1993 diplomatic oral history describes briefings and his responsibilities involving a U.S. Air Force tracking facility in Seychelles. His description gives an agency-records researcher a posting, period and subject to search. [ADST oral history](https://adst.org/OH%20TOCs/CARLSON%2C%20Richard%20W.toc.pdf) · [Seychelles appointment chronology](seychelles.md)

**His sons’ accounts.** On Shawn Ryan’s show in 2024, Tucker described his father working with the CIA. In a later interview, Buckley characterized Richard’s work as adjacent and denied direct employment. Record the speaker, date, and exact capacity claimed. An appointment, a liaison assignment, and contact during diplomatic service are distinct propositions.
