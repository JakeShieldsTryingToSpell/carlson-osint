# The 2019 Rockbridge gathering

The Washington Post reported that Tucker Carlson attended the 2019 gathering in Ohio and described JD Vance and Peter Thiel as its conveners. The article's opening attributes the guest list to two people familiar with the meeting. Carlson's attendance is recorded as **reported**. ## Source audit

- **Attendance and convening.** Elizabeth Dwoskin's November 4, 2025 article describes the gathering in the opening and returns to it under “A coordination problem.” Both passages describe the conveners in the reporter’s narration. Blake Masters is quoted about the discussion in the second passage, which also describes him meeting Chris Buskirk that weekend. The reviewed attribution for Carlson's attendance is in the opening account. The Daily Beast and Capital Research Center credit the Post for their accounts of the Ohio gathering. [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2025/11/04/chris-buskirk-maga-vance-post-trump/) · [The Daily Beast, opening paragraphs](https://www.thedailybeast.com/secret-gop-cabal-with-vance-and-thiel-plots-eternal-maga-rule) · [Capital Research Center, “Presuppositions, old and new, and perceptivity”](https://capitalresearch.org/article/philanthropy-and-furious-minds/)
- **Founding and formal roles.** In August 2024, Reuters reported that Vance and Buskirk launched the network in 2019, citing a prospectus distributed ahead of an April 2024 retreat. Reuters identified Vance and Buskirk as co-founders and described Vance's later ties as informal. The prospectus attribution comes through Reuters. The Post describes Buskirk as leading the network in 2025. Carlson's formal role in the organization remains unresolved. [Reuters, co-founder and prospectus passages](https://www.inc.com/reuters/vances-tech-donor-network-wants-to-push-us-to-right.html) · [The Washington Post, opening account](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2025/11/04/chris-buskirk-maga-vance-post-trump/)
- **Other events.** In October 2023, Puck described Carlson as the keynote speaker at the network's post-election Austin summit the previous year, placing that appearance in 2022. The July 2019 National Conservatism program lists Carlson and Thiel as speakers at a separate event in Washington, D.C. Use each source with its own venue and date.
