# The Vaughan estate

The California appeal traces earlier distributions from Mary Nickel James’s estate and a later dispute about Lisa Vaughan’s California mineral interests. Read the factual history, the competing accounts, and the disposition as distinct parts of the opinion. [Appellate opinion, F077628](https://www.casemine.com/judgement/us/5cff6fad8a6bc868cbd405cd?locale=en_US)
