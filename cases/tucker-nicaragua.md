# Tucker Carlson’s time in Nicaragua

What can be dated about Carlson’s visits, who traveled with him, and what purpose did he or contemporary witnesses give?

| Date | What the archive has |
| --- | --- |
| 1988 | In a 2000 byline, Carlson recalled being in Managua in 1988. His account gives a year and place. The itinerary and purpose remain open. [Carlson’s byline](https://weeklystandardarchives.com/articles/ho-ho-ho-chi-minh-city/) |
| Early 1990 | Later accounts cite a March issue of Trinity College’s *Tripod* describing Carlson and Neil Patel at election rallies. The archive has the old catalog pointer and a quotation reproduced later. The scan, headline, byline and page are still missing. [Legacy locator](https://digitalrepository.trincoll.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1122&context=tripod) · [Reproduced quotation](https://archive.ph/ZEIst) |
| April 1990 | A later profile describes a campus presentation. A dated program, announcement or recording would establish when it occurred and what the participants said. [Account of the campus talk](https://www.yahoo.com/news/tucker-carlson-origin-story-113441050.html) |

The election trip and the 1988 recollection have different dates and sources. A claim about operational activity would need evidence identifying the activity and Carlson’s role.
