[Original reporting](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2025/11/04/chris-buskirk-maga-vance-post-trump/) · [2019 event brief](rockbridge.md) |
| 2022, Austin | Puck reported a keynote appearance at a later summit. Check the date, venue, speaker billing and organizer against an event record. [Puck reporting](https://puck.news/washington/2023/10/rockbridge-network-over-troubled-waters) |

Speaking at a summit, convening it, funding it and holding an office require different records. An agenda can identify a speaker. An appointment, governance document or attributable organizer statement could identify an organizational role.
