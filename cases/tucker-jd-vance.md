# Tucker Carlson and JD Vance

When and how did Carlson give Vance airtime, publicly back him, or advocate for him during a political decision?

**Airtime.** Media Matters reported 46 appearances during the Fox show’s run. The archive’s dated ledger separately lists 29 Fox cable interviews and three interviews on other platforms. A comparable count needs the episode, platform, host, and whether it was a new airing.
