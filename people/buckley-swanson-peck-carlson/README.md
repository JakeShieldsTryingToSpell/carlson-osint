# Buckley Swanson Peck Carlson

## Connection to Tucker

Tucker’s brother. The California estate opinion names Richard Carlson and Lisa Lombardi Vaughan as their parents.

## Key findings

- **From polling to broadcasting.** A June 2007 industry profile traces his work at Luntz Research Companies and McCarthy Communications, his production role on *Danger Zone*, and his recruitment by Kelton Research. [Contemporary industry profile](https://www.mrweb.com/drno/printable/pn6866.htm)

- **Corporate messaging.** In *Words That Work*, Frank Luntz credits Buckley with language used in a corporate-governance campaign for an unnamed health-care company. The account identifies the assignment and leaves the client unnamed. [Book, pp. 146–147](https://www.apdoo.org/judyweiss/Luntz%20words%20that%20work.pdf)
