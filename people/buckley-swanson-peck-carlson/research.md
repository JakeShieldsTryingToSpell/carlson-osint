## Broadcasting and political communications

- **Buckley’s producer credit.** A September 22, 2005 *Washington Examiner* column names Buckley as WMAL’s *Danger Zone* producer and places both Carlson brothers at *The Weekly Standard*’s tenth-anniversary event. A June 2007 industry report says Buckley left McCarthy Communications in March 2005 to become executive producer of the ABC Radio program and was still producing it when Kelton Research hired him. The public credits establish his production role. His contracting entity and any direct FDD employment remain open. [2005 column](https://www.karenfeld.com/monica-partying-two-continents/) · [2007 industry reporting](https://www.mrweb.com/drno/printable/pn6866.htm)

**Career sequence.** In the January 2026 interview, Buckley says he spent about six years on Frank Luntz’s team and helped run it. MrWeb identifies him as a vice president of Luntz Research Companies, then executive vice president at McCarthy Communications, then executive producer of *Danger Zone* beginning in March 2005. Kelton Research recruited him in June 2007 to lead its Washington expansion while he continued producing the show. MrWeb independently mentions two presidential campaigns, speechwriting for California Gov. Pete Wilson and work around the 1997–98 tobacco policy fights. Exact Luntz start and end dates remain open. [Full interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ir_7ll4nEVM) · [Luntz chapter](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ir_7ll4nEVM&t=5843s) · [Interview transcript](https://podcasts.happyscribe.com/the-tucker-carlson-show/tucker-s-brother-buckley-carlson-on-dogs-childhood-nicotine-frank-luntz-and-america-s-future) · [2007 industry reporting](https://www.mrweb.com/drno/printable/pn6866.htm) · [Paul Leslie interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i0-z5KMqjc4)

The case study gives examples of the messaging. It uses “corporate democracy” for the concept Buckley was asked to counter and provides no attribution for coining that phrase. | Purdue Pharma and the Sacklers | In the full interview, Tucker raises the Purdue/Sackler work and Buckley acknowledges involvement and expresses regret. Buckley also recalls seeing Luntz work with Pfizer and Coca-Cola. The exchange does not specify a contract, campaign deliverables or the dates of Buckley’s Purdue work. The SEC-governance client in the book is unnamed and cannot be identified from this exchange.
