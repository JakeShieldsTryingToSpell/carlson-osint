# Peter Lombardi

## Connection to Tucker

Peter was Tucker’s maternal uncle. The California estate opinion identifies him as Lisa Lombardi Vaughan’s brother and Tucker as her son.

## Key findings

Peter appears in the court’s account of the will proceedings. [Vaughan estate case](../../cases/vaughan-estate.md)
