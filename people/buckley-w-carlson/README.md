# Buckley W. Carlson

## Connection to Tucker

Tucker and Susan Andrews Carlson’s son. His uncle, [Buckley Swanson Peck Carlson](../buckley-swanson-peck-carlson/README.md), appears in the Vaughan estate case.

## Key findings

- **Capitol Hill to the vice president’s office.** House records list him on Jim Banks’s staff, including as deputy chief of staff in 2023. In January 2025, ABC reported his appointment as JD Vance’s deputy press secretary. [House exhibit](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/gtimages/ST/2023/500027155.pdf) · [ABC appointment reporting](https://abcnews.com/US/tucker-carlsons-son-buckley-joining-jd-vances-staff/story?id=118150708)

- **Political and media access.** Reporting traced Tucker’s public coverage of Banks during Buckley’s employment. The research notes give the dated appearances, Banks’s account of the hire, and the limits of what the sources establish. [Banks coverage and public response](https://www.vice.com/en/article/tucker-carlson-keeps-interviewing-a-congressman-who-is-also-his-sons-boss/)
