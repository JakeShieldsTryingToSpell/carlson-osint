# Lisa McNear Lombardi Vaughan

## Connection to Tucker

Lisa was the biological mother of Tucker and his brother Buckley. The California Court of Appeal records her marriage to Richard Carlson and their later divorce.

## Key findings

The Vaughan estate appeal traces the dispute over her California mineral interests. [Vaughan estate case](../../cases/vaughan-estate.md)
