# Patricia Swanson Carlson

## Connection to Tucker

Patricia married Tucker’s father, Richard Carlson. The estate opinion expressly says she adopted Tucker and his brother Buckley.

## Key findings

- **Corporate role.** State company data identifies Patricia as a vice president of Tulip Hill Enterprises Incorporated. [Registry record](https://cis.scc.virginia.gov/EntitySearch/BusinessInformation?businessId=05113022) · [State-data mirror](https://vabusinesses.org/business/05113022)

- **Joint contribution.** FDD’s 2004 filing lists Patricia and Richard together as $60,000 contributors. It gives one amount for the couple. [Schedule B, contributor 16, PDF p. 17](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/display_990/134174402/2005_11_PF%2F13-4174402_990PF_200412)
