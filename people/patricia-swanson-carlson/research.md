## Tulip Hill Enterprises and the FDD return

The Virginia corporate record for entity 05113022 and the state-data mirror identify Patricia as a vice president of Tulip Hill. The 2004 FDD return lists Patricia and Richard together for $60,000 on Schedule B, contributor 16. The filing provides a joint amount and leaves individual shares and the purpose of the gift unspecified.
