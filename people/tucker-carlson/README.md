# Tucker Carlson

## Connection to Tucker

Tucker is the reference point for this family tree. The California estate opinion names his biological parents, Richard Carlson and Lisa Lombardi Vaughan, and records his adoption by Patricia Swanson Carlson.

## Key findings

- **Peter Thiel.** Carlson recalled receiving business advice from Thiel in 2009. Fox carried two interviews in July 2019. The date of their first meeting and any role Thiel played in financing Carlson’s media company remain open. [Straka, 1:28:12–1:28:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kckVByHcgsk&t=5269s) · [July 15 broadcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rrfJUS7iOYE) · [July 30 Fox transcript](https://www.foxnews.com/transcript/peter-thiel-calls-for-top-universities-to-lose-non-profit-status)

- **Hungarian government access.** A 2019 DOJ filing says Policy Impact coordinated the Hungarian foreign minister’s interview on Carlson’s show. The firm reported the work. The filing leaves the individual booker and any payment to Carlson unidentified. [FARA supplemental filing, item 11](https://efile.fara.gov/docs/6544-Supplemental-Statement-20190515-1.pdf)

- **JD Vance’s airtime.** Media Matters reported 46 appearances before Carlson’s Fox show ended. The notebook’s dated ledger identifies 29 Fox cable interviews and three on other platforms. The full roster behind the published tally remains an open record. [Published tally](https://www.mediamatters.org/fox-news/jd-vance-donald-trumps-vp-pick-owes-everything-tucker-carlson) · [Appearance ledger](research.md#published-count-and-dated-audit)
