# Tucker Carlson

## Connection to Tucker

Tucker is the reference point for this family tree. The California estate opinion names his biological parents, Richard Carlson and Lisa Lombardi Vaughan, and records his adoption by Patricia Swanson Carlson.

## Key findings

On July 15, 2019, Tucker interviewed Peter Thiel about Google, the day after Thiel’s scheduled Washington keynote. [Fox broadcast](https://www.foxbusiness.com/video/6059543095001) · [Conference program](https://nationalconservatism.org/natcon-dc-2019/conference-schedule/)

The Washington Post reported that Tucker attended the 2019 Ohio gathering convened by Peter Thiel and JD Vance. Its account cites two people familiar with the meeting. His precise role remains open. [Rockbridge case](../../cases/rockbridge.md)
