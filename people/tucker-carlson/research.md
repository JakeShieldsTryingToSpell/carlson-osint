## Peter Thiel and the July 2019 broadcast

The National Conservatism program scheduled Peter Thiel for July 14 and Tucker Carlson for July 15 in Washington, D.C. Fox published their on-air exchange on July 15. These records establish a shared program and a direct public interview. The date of their first private contact and any part either played in the other’s financial or editorial decisions are separate questions. [Conference schedule](https://nationalconservatism.org/natcon-dc-2019/conference-schedule/) · [Fox broadcast](https://www.foxbusiness.com/video/6059543095001)
