## Peter Thiel — what Tucker said and what the records show

- **Carlson dates one private exchange to 2009.** On Brandon Straka’s November 23, 2026 show, he said Thiel gave him business advice in 2009, when he was considering how to start a business. Contemporary reporting puts Carlson and Neil Patel at work on what became *The Daily Caller* that year. The overlap makes the Caller the leading identification, with the name of the business, the channel of communication and the date and circumstances of their first meeting still open. The advice is Carlson’s account, given 17 years later. [Straka, 1:28:12–1:28:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kckVByHcgsk&t=5269s) · [Washingtonian, Dec. 11, 2009](https://washingtonian.com/2009/12/11/tuckers-next-move/)

- **Fox gave Thiel at least two interviews with Carlson in July 2019.** The July 15 broadcast extended Thiel’s NatCon charge about Google and China to a national audience. A separate July 30 interview gave him a forum to challenge elite universities’ tax exemptions. Carlson treated both arguments sympathetically. Thiel had other routes to the public and to President Trump. The appearances establish access and favorable treatment of these arguments. The booking correspondence and any evidence of an editorial bargain remain unavailable. [NatCon program](https://nationalconservatism.org/natcon-dc-2019/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2019/07/NC-Conference-Program-v6.pdf) · [July 15 broadcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rrfJUS7iOYE) · [July 30 Fox transcript](https://www.foxnews.com/transcript/peter-thiel-calls-for-top-universities-to-lose-non-profit-status) · [Trump, July 16](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-201900474/pdf/DCPD-201900474.pdf)

- **The closest political overlap is a reported 2019 planning summit followed by separately sourced accounts of lobbying for Vance.** The *Washington Post* places both men at an Ohio gathering reportedly convened by Thiel and JD Vance. FEC receipts put $15 million from Thiel into a pro-Vance PAC. Fox records put Carlson behind Vance publicly, and original reporting puts Carlson and Thiel in overlapping efforts to persuade Trump in 2024. Carlson also reportedly keynoted a Rockbridge summit in 2022. The public record leaves a shared call, message relay between them, or division of work unresolved. [Ohio summit reporting](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2025/11/04/chris-buskirk-maga-vance-post-trump/) · [Austin keynote reporting](https://puck.news/washington/2023/10/rockbridge-network-over-troubled-waters) · [FEC receipts, Mar. 2021 and Apr. 2022](https://docquery.fec.gov/cgi-bin/fecimg/?202107309452400203) [FEC 2022](https://docquery.fec.gov/cgi-bin/fecimg/?202207119518356187) · [Fox, July 1, 2021](https://www.foxnews.com/transcript/tucker-people-in-charge-create-disaster-after-disaster) · [CNN, July 15, 2024](https://ktvz.com/politics/cnn-us-politics/2024/07/15/how-donald-trump-landed-on-jd-vance-as-his-vice-presidential-pick/) · [Post, July 28, 2024](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2024/07/28/jd-vance-peter-thiel-donors-big-tech-trump-vp/)

- **The main financial question is who actually funded the 1789 investment and what they received in return.** In August 2023 CNBC reported that Carlson’s side approached Thiel and that Thiel signaled possible interest to allies. In October the *Wall Street Journal* broke the 1789-led financing story. The approximately $15 million deal, reported SAFE and March 2025 exit are substantially supported. Axios’s June 2025 account attributes the bulk of the round to 1789 and refers to other investors, so the exact 1789 check is uncertain. Neither a signed SAFE nor an allocation or payout schedule surfaced. Those records would identify the purchaser, any Thiel-funded allocation and any rights over the company or its output. [CNBC, Aug. 8, 2023](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/08/08/tucker-carlson-may-see-rebekah-mercer-peter-thiel-invest-in-company.html) · [WSJ, Oct. 17, 2023](https://www.wsj.com/business/media/tucker-carlsons-media-company-secures-investment-led-by-new-anti-woke-firm-1789-capital-a47d20dd) · [TechCrunch, Oct. 21, 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/21/tucker-carlson-last-country-media-startup/) · [Axios, June 13, 2025](https://www.axios.com/2025/06/13/tucker-carlson-investors) · [Reuters, Sept. 8, 2025](https://www.reuters.com/investigations/trump-linked-venture-fund-1789-capital-tops-1-billion-assets-2025-09-08/)

- **Carlson criticized surveillance involving Palantir and understated Thiel’s governance role.** The original Straka video captions assign the question about Thiel being “just an investor” to Carlson. Carlson correctly identified Alex Karp as the executive running the company and described grave concern about surveillance and military uses. The company’s 2026 proxy identifies Thiel as co-founder and chair since 2003, with board oversight and participation in a founder voting arrangement. The independent-media sample contains other express criticism of surveillance involving Palantir. Evidence tying any coverage decision to Thiel remains unresolved. [Straka, 1:28:50–1:29:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kckVByHcgsk&t=5330s) · [Palantir 2026 proxy, board leadership and founder voting](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1321655/000132165526000019/pltr-20260423.htm) · [Carlson and Ana Kasparian, July 25, 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uyh62MUEC3o)

## Executive summary

- **Trinity College and Nicaragua.** Contemporary career reporting identifies Carlson as a 1991 Trinity history graduate. A reproduced senior yearbook page documents his entry and its references to the Dan White Society and Jesse Helms Foundation. Carlson independently put himself in Managua in 1988 in a 2000 byline. Two later accounts cite a March 1990 student-paper item about Carlson and Neil Patel in Nicaragua during the election. One preserves the legacy PDF locator. The campus issue image, the April Faculty Club program or recording, and any evidence of an operational role with the Contras remain outstanding. ## 1. Education, early life, and career

### Schools and family context

The strongest readily accessible graduation evidence is the August 1999 *Washington Post* profile, written while Carlson was a thirty-year-old magazine journalist. It reports a 1991 history degree and attributes the low college average to Carlson. Cato's February 2009 announcement and the Independent Institute's speaker biography independently publish the history-degree description, although neither presents a registrar's record. A 2026 Trump statement said Carlson did not finish college. It supplies no credentials record and conflicts with the 1999 reporting. A senior yearbook entry corroborates class association. A commencement program, a contemporaneous degree list or confirmation from the registrar would establish conferral independently of profiles. [1999 profile](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/lifestyle/1999/08/17/the-opinionated-journalist/4340b332-e33d-442b-b681-fbb97cffee71/) · [Cato](https://www.cato.org/blog/tucker-carlson-joins-cato-institute) · [Independent Institute](https://www.independent.org/person/tucker-carlson/) · [Trump statement reported in April 2026](https://www.thedailybeast.com/vance-loses-top-aide-buckley-carlson-amid-trump-iran-war-feud-with-tucker) · [Yearbook image](https://www.thewrap.com/tucker-carlson-dan-white-yearbook/)

Carlson attended St. George's in Middletown, Rhode Island, where the reporting places his relationship with his future wife and his early political debating. California profiles place his childhood in La Jolla. The school-name search produced unrelated St. George's institutions, including a Swiss school with its own yearbook archive. Its holdings cannot identify the Rhode Island student. The reviewed public sources leave the full earlier-school sequence, individual school-year dates and any overseas enrollment unresolved. [1999 profile](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/lifestyle/1999/08/17/the-opinionated-journalist/4340b332-e33d-442b-b681-fbb97cffee71/) · [2021 retrospective](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/tucker-carlson/2021/07/13/398fa720-dd9f-11eb-a501-0e69b5d012e5_story.html) · [Rhode Island school's archives entry point](https://www.stgeorges.edu/academics/academic-facilities/library/archives)

The reproduced *Ivy* senior entry lists Christian Fellowship, the Jesse Helms Foundation and the “Dan White Society.” Dan White killed San Francisco Mayor George Moscone and Supervisor Harvey Milk in 1978. Trinity's spokesperson said they could not explain the 1991 submission process. The page establishes the wording. Evidence that a functioning organization existed, what the phrase meant to its submitter, and who wrote the entry requires corroboration from the full volume, club records or independent classmates. Later coverage reproducing the same page is a second publication of one artifact. [Yearbook reproduction and Trinity comment](https://www.thewrap.com/tucker-carlson-dan-white-yearbook/)

The opinion links a preserved episode transcript at pp. 4–5 and explains its contextual analysis at pp. 9–13. Its holding concerns the specific allegations in this case. The Fox parties disputed intent and offered a different account of their coverage. The April 18 settlement for $787.5 million closed the trial path and Fox acknowledged the court's falsity rulings.
