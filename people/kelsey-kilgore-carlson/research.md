## 4. Lobbying records and policy tests

### How to read the ledger

Kimbell is the **registrant and reported income recipient** on the matched LD-2s. The named client is the party on whose behalf the firm reports work. Line 12 is a combined firm-level amount for the client and filing period. Line 18 is the activity-page roster and line 17 the chambers or agencies reported for that issue. Each source below shows a UUID and a signature date or filing-period metadata. Legislative lobbying reports generally do not enumerate meetings, employees’ pay, decision authority or each lobbyist’s individual contacts. A filer appearing as the contact on page 1 is a separate signal. The public results interface and filtered API could not be enumerated here. The table highlights material items.
