# Kelsey Kilgore Carlson

## Connection to Tucker

Kelsey is Tucker’s daughter-in-law through his son Buckley W. Carlson. LegiStorm identifies her as Buckley’s spouse.

## Key findings

- **White House policy work.** The 2019 personnel report lists Kilgore as a policy adviser detailed to the White House at an annual rate of $57,510. A workforce advisory-board paper credits her with project coordination. [White House payroll, PDF p. 9](https://trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/July-1-2019-Report-FINAL.pdf#page=9) · [Workforce advisory paper, p. 2](https://credentialengine.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/ILR_White_Paper_FINAL-EBOOK.pdf#page=4)

- **Lobbying disclosures.** Kimbell filings name her on health IT, Medicare, and tariff issues. The SSI Group filing lists the NSC among the bodies contacted by the lobbying team. It leaves the individual contact and outcome unspecified. [SSI Group disclosure](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/2103e59f-d1be-404b-b073-336bb916433a/print/) · [Accuray tariff disclosure](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/32dd0be6-e7c4-4569-86ba-6f4a69951833/print/)
