# Richard Warner Carlson

## Connection to Tucker

Richard, also known as Dick Carlson, was the biological father of Tucker and his brother Buckley. The California estate opinion identifies both sons.

## Key findings

- **FDD compensation and company payments.** The notebook’s year-by-year transcriptions total $1,002,124 in personal compensation and $110,455 in benefits or other compensation for 2003–11. Separately, filings report $2,098,273 in payments and reimbursements to Tulip Hill for 2005–11. [Returns index](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/134174402) · [2011 Schedule L](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/display_990/134174402/2012_12_EO%2F13-4174402_990_201112) · [Annual ledger](research.md#fdd-compensation-and-tulip-hill-transactions)

- **Public accounts of intelligence work.** Tucker publicly described Richard working with the CIA. The role, dates, and employment status remain unresolved. The notebook places that statement beside Richard’s own diplomatic oral history and Buckley’s account. [Shawn Ryan interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uElaHUSM7fI&t=3050s) · [ADST oral history](https://adst.org/OH%20TOCs/CARLSON%2C%20Richard%20W.toc.pdf) · [Buckley interview](https://fight.fudgie.org/search/show/cha/episode/20261120_Fri__4SQgLfo6_E)

- **Government service.** Richard directed Voice of America and later served as ambassador to Seychelles. The State Department records his appointment on July 2, 1991 and his departure on July 5, 1992. [Office of the Historian](https://history.state.gov/departmenthistory/people/carlson-richard-w)
