# Richard Warner Carlson

## Connection to Tucker

Richard, also known as Dick Carlson, was the biological father of Tucker and his brother Buckley. The California estate opinion identifies both sons.

## Key findings

The State Department dates his appointment as U.S. ambassador to Seychelles to July 2, 1991. He presented credentials on October 1 and left the post on July 5, 1992. [State Department record](https://history.state.gov/departmenthistory/people/carlson-richard-w)
