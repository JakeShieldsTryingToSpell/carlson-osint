## Intelligence agencies and diplomatic service

In a June 3, 2024 interview with Shawn Ryan, Tucker described his father working with the CIA. The passage is a family member’s account. It leaves the dates, capacity and activity unspecified. Richard’s own March 2, 1993 diplomatic oral history covers his Voice of America and Seychelles work and discusses relations with U.S. agencies. Compare the two accounts with contemporary agency or USIA records. Record exactly whose conduct each passage describes.
